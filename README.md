# Claxon's Cupcakes 🧁

A simple website for ordering homemade cupcakes from Claxon's Cupcakes in South Webster, Ohio.

## What's on the Site
* **Flavors & Menu:** Shows current cupcake options ($25/dozen).
* **Easy Ordering:** Fill out the order form and click submit to open a pre-filled text message sent straight to the phone for easy ordering.
* **Payment Options:** Information for Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or cash on pickup.
* **About:** A short background on the baker.

## Menu Options ($25 / Dozen)
* Cookie Dough
* Ho-Ho
* Strawberry Cheesecake
* Orange Dreamsicle
* Strawberry Shortcake
